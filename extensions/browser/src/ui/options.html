Clipping

Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+L opens the clipper, Alt+Shift+P clips the page, Alt+Shift+S the selection, Alt+Shift+K picks. Change them in your browser's extension shortcuts.

The assistant

Ask Lens from its side panel: Ask Lens in the popup or on the right-click menu. While the panel is open, the assistant can open, read and act on pages in that window, only on sites you allowed, and it asks you before anything that sends, deletes, buys or posts.
